SurvivalBD

A survival game on a deserted island. Shipwrecked, alone, with a shadow that comes at night. You make a fire, find water and food, go down into the mountain's cave, keep Brother Ioan's canon and, at the end, build a raft to sail home.

A game by Bran David Ionel · Unity 2021.3 · phone (Android) and PC (Windows)

The story

The game begins on the beach, next to the wreck of the ship. On a black screen the story appears line by line (it can be skipped with Skip). It is shown only the first time on a device: after that, or if there are saved games already, a new game starts right away with level 1.

The storm came out of nowhere.

For three nights the waves threw our ship around, until it broke apart on the rocks.

I remember the cold water... the screams... and then nothing.

When I opened my eyes, I was lying on the sand. Alone.

The ship is gone. The others are gone. I have nothing but my hands.

Night will come. First of all, I need a fire.

The player starts with empty hands, at 7 in the morning. A shadow lives on the island: a tall black figure with white eyes that comes at night when no fire burns nearby. In the mountain's cave is the place where Brother Ioan died, a monk who lived on the island long ago and fought the same shadow. He left a journal, a prayer book and a canon. The prayer drives the shadow away.

The whole game is in English or in Romanian (Settings, or the two buttons at the top right of the main menu): the story, the goals, the messages, the items, the windows and the buttons.

The levels

The goals of the current level are in the panel at the top right. A goal that is ticked stays ticked. At the end of each level "LEVEL N COMPLETE" appears and the story goes on. Level 10 (the raft) always stays the last one: new levels go before it.

1
Fă un foc

Make a fire

  1. Gather 6 stones (on the beach and in the grass).
  2. Make a campfire: Crafting › Survival › Campfire (6 stones).
  3. Place the fire (from the quick bar or the inventory).
  4. Find wood (branches / sticks) and hay (pull dry grass on the meadows).
  5. Light the fire: wood in the fire + 1 hay.
"The fire crackles. Its warmth gives me hope."
2
Apă dulce

Fresh water

  1. Find the river that comes down from the mountain, or the lake it flows into.
  2. Drink: look down at the water and Drink water appears.
The sea is salty: never drink from it. After rain the puddles are drinking water too (2–3 days).
3
Ceva de mâncare

Something to eat

  1. Pick 2 mushrooms in the forest, in the shade of the trees.
  2. Eat them (in the inventory: double tap on the phone / right click on the PC).
Porcini are the best. Berries and herbs help a little too.
4
Prima noapte

The first night

The story tells that something strange is on the island: a dark shape watched from between the trees and kept away from the fire. Much more wood is needed.

  1. Gather firewood: 3 logs or 8 sticks.
  2. Have the fire burning when night falls.
  3. Stay by the fire until morning.
The stone axe: 1 stone + 2 sticks. Logs burn the longest.
5
Muntele

The mountain

At night the ground shook, and at dawn stones were heard falling on the mountain. The cave, closed until now by rocks, opens.

  1. Explore the mountain.
  2. Find what the fallen rocks opened (the mouth of the cave).
Inside it is pitch dark: no going in without light. A column of light shows the cave's mouth.
6
În întuneric

Into the dark

  1. Make a torch: Crafting › Tools › Torch (1 stick + 2 hay).
  2. Hold the torch in your hand (it lights up).
  3. Go down to the end of the cave.
  4. Read Brother Ioan's old journal.
  5. Take the prayer book.
Danger A torch burns about 6 game hours in the hand. If you are in the cave without light (or the torch goes out), the shadow takes you after a few seconds. Take a spare torch.
7
Rugăciunea

The prayer

  1. Wait for the night.
  2. When the shadow comes, open the prayer book.
The book has 113 official Orthodox prayers (in English or Romanian, as chosen in the Settings) and drives the shadow away for a few hours. From now on you have three defences: the fire, a burning torch in your hand and the prayer.
8
Fierul

Iron

  1. Find 10 iron ore in the cave (with the torch!). There are about 50 in the tunnel.
  2. Have a log.
  3. Make the iron axe: Crafting › Tools › Iron Axe (10 ore + 1 log).
With the iron axe any tree falls in 3 blows (with the stone axe the big trees take up to 8).
9
Canonul fratelui Ioan

Brother Ioan's canon

Among Brother Ioan's pages you find a canon written in his own hand: "Do not spoil what is beautiful on this island. Care for the creatures and they will multiply; plant trees for those who come after you."

"You must make paradise out of everything you have."

"And when your work is done, raise a cross on the other mountain." He kept it, and God kept him alive on the island; with His help you keep it too.

  1. Catch a male and a female rabbit alive: Crafting › Survival › Snare (1 stick + 5 hemp). Set the snare near rabbits; a grown rabbit that walks into it is caught. With empty hands you take it alive into the inventory, with the axe you kill it.
  2. Build a pen: a fence all around with a gate (Crafting › Construction › Fence / Gate). The yard can be as big as you like and of any shape: each new panel joins the end of the last one, and one put between two free ends closes the yard. (A house works too: foundation, walls, a door wall and a roof.)
  3. Go into the pen and let the male and the female go (drag out of the inventory, or double tap / right click). The goal is done at once and stays done, even if they go out later. With the gate shut they stay in the pen and multiply (a pair has 2–5 babies about a day later).
  4. Plant 10 trees (pine cones, acorns, beechnuts, birch catkins).
  5. Make the cross: Crafting › Construction › Cross (5 planks).
  6. Last: raise the cross high on the other mountain (the north-east one), on the flat stone where Brother Ioan's steps end (see Menu › Map and the column of light).
The miracle: water springs from the rock under the cross and a new stream runs down to the sea on the north coast. You can drink from it. Now you understand where the other river came from: Brother Ioan raised a cross there too. He was a saint, and you feel that you are protected as well.
The gate: if you leave it open, the rabbits in the pen go out one after another. A fence with a gap is not a pen. A grown rabbit in the pen can be taken alive with empty hands (good for the raft). A killed baby gives nothing; half grown it gives only a little meat; grown rabbits give meat and hide.
Winter: one game day after the miracle, winter comes and snow falls over the whole island. Keep a fire burning at night.
10
Pluta · the last level

The raft

The raft, only started, lies on the beach where the new stream flows into the sea (the north coast). A blue column of light shows the place, and the gold ring on the map (Menu › Map). Look at it and click: everything it needs that you carry goes onto it. Make as many trips as you like.

  1. Pick 18 hemp (most of it grows around the lake).
  2. Make 6 ropes: Crafting › Refine › Rope (3 hemp each).
  3. Go to the raft, at the mouth of the new stream.
  4. Bring it 12 logs, 8 planks, 6 ropes, 16 sticks, 10 hay, 5 cooked meat, 3 rabbit hides and 2 live rabbits (from the pen: set a snare inside it, or from the snares).
  5. Done, the raft slides into the sea. Board it.
The end: the raft sails towards the sunset, the island gets smaller behind it, the last lines of the story appear, then "THE END – SurvivalBD, a game by Bran David Ionel – Thank you for playing!" and the game goes back to the main menu.

Survival

Time

A game day lasts 6 real minutes (one game hour = 15 seconds). There are seasons, rain, fog and snow; in winter snow covers the ground and some plants.

Water 1 day

A full water bar lasts one game day. Drink from the river, the lake, the new stream of the canon and the puddles after rain (they dry up after 2–3 days). The sea: Sea water (salty), it can't be drunk.

Food ~3 days

A full food bar lasts about 3 game days. Walking and chopping wood use it up faster. Mushrooms, berries, herbs and meat fill it.

Health

When water or food reach zero, health goes down until death (with warnings first). Mushrooms and herbs give health too. The bars at the top of the screen are big and show the numbers: Health, Food, Water.

Fire

A campfire = 6 stones. To light it put wood in it (sticks, logs, planks) and use 1 hay. The wood burns away; logs last the longest.

Settings

Language: English or Romanian, from the main menu (the buttons at the top right) or Menu › Settings. It changes the whole game: the story, the levels, the journal, the prayers, the messages, the items, the windows, the buttons and the end.

Menu › Settings: the volume of the nature sounds, of the effects and the main volume, and the difficulty: Easy (food and water last longer, torches burn longer, the shadow is slower), Normal or Hard (hunger and thirst come faster, torches go out sooner, the shadow is quick).

Saving

Menu › Save Game: 10 save slots, each with a picture. The save keeps the level, the trees, the picked plants, the rabbits, the fires, the puddles, the things dropped on the ground, the buildings (with their doors), the snares and the raft.

While loading, the save's picture is shown with a random Orthodox prayer written in red (at least 6 seconds, so you can read it).

Dropping things

Drag an item out of the inventory outside the window: the game asks whether to drop it. Yes: it falls in front of you and you can take it again any time. On the trash can the item is gone for good (it asks here too). A live rabbit dropped is let go: it runs away, unless it is in a closed pen.

The map

Menu › Map (key N on the PC): a picture of the island from above with a red arrow for you (the way you look) and a gold ring on the place of the current goal (the cave, the other mountain, the raft...).

The shadow

night When it is dark and no fire burns within 20 m, after a few seconds the shadow appears some 40 m away and comes towards you. You have about 20 seconds: light a fire or open the prayer book. If it catches you, you die and the game goes back to the main menu.

the cave The cave is pitch dark and the shadow lives there. Without a burning torch in your hand, more than a few metres from the entrance: a warning, heartbeats, then "The shadow took you in the dark...".

defence The fire (while it burns), a burning torch in your hand (anywhere, not only in the cave) and the prayer book (it drives the shadow away for a few hours each time you open it at night).

The island

An oval island about 2 km wide, with a mountain in the middle and a lake in its crater.

The mountains have real rock on their steep slopes (patches, layers of stone and relief), with a little grass left on the gentler parts high up. On the top of the big mountain stands Brother Ioan's old cross, of dark wood. The map (Menu › Map, key N) is a picture of the island from above, with your arrow and the level's goal.

The beach and the wreck

Where the game begins. Pieces of the ship, planks and driftwood, stones.

The forests

Four kinds of trees: pine, birch, oak and beech. Under the trees the ground is dark, without grass, with ferns and bushes, mushrooms, fallen branches, logs and old stumps.

The trees grow by themselves

The trees drop their seeds (cones, acorns, beechnuts, catkins). Fallen seeds sprout after 3–4 game days and become new trees (if their spot is taken, they find a free one next to it). You can pick them and plant them yourself. A felled tree leaves logs, branches (few from pines, more from the others), a stump and, if it was big, 2–4 seeds from its crown.

Brother Ioan's paths

Over many years Brother Ioan made the island's paths with his own hands: from the beach where the fishermen left him (by the wreck) to the lake, to his cave in the mountain, up the other mountain and down to the northern shore. Where the rock is too steep to walk he cut stone steps (up to the cave's mouth and up the other mountain's ridge), and at the top he laid a flat stone for the cross. No tree grows on them.

The meadows

Lots of grass: Pick grass (hay) gives hay (the same spot can be picked again after a day). Flowers, chicory, heather, berry bushes.

The river, the lake and the new stream

The river starts high on the big mountain (under Brother Ioan's cross) and flows into the crater lake. After the canon a new stream runs from the other mountain to the north coast. You can drink from all of them. Hemp grows thickest around the lake.

The mountain and the cave

The cave goes down a long way into the mountain: stalactites, stalagmites, mushrooms (some of them glow), iron ore. At the end: an Orthodox cross, the monk's bones, candles, the journal and the prayer book.

The rabbits

More than 100 rabbits all over the island. Above each one is written Male (blue) or Female (pink); the babies grow up in a day and a half. A male and a female that see each other 40 m away come together (a little heart shows) and have 2–5 babies a day later. Where many rabbits live already no more babies come; in a pen they always multiply. Hunted with the axe they leave meat and a hide; caught in a snare they can be taken alive and kept in a pen.
