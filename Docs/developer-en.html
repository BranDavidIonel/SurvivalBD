What is in the scene (GameScene)

The forests, the plants, the rabbits, the river and the cave are ordinary objects in the scene: you see them in the Hierarchy and move, delete or duplicate them by hand.

Made in code when the game runs (not in the scene): the living forest (Forest (made in code); far away the trees are pictures, close by they are models: 110 m on the phone, 170 m on the PC), the story, the rabbits, the miracle stream of the canon (its way to the sea is found on the terrain), the raft of level 10 (at the mouth of the new stream) and the pieces of the pen (door wall, window wall, roof).

The tools change the scene. Save the scene after them; if you don't like the result, you can undo it with git.

Quick testing

In the game: Menu › Settings, in the Admin row type the password 1212 and press Unlock (then Admin settings). The TEST panel stays unlocked until you close the game; players don't see it without the password. There you can change the hour (sunrise, day, sunset, night), the speed of time (x1…x300), the walking speed, the weather and the season. For the levels:

  • Next level – finishes the current level and goes to the next one.
  • Give items – gives stones, sticks, hay, a log and a mushroom.
  • Give item – any item of the game (from the list), 1 or 5 at a time.
  • Immortal – you never lose health, food or water and nothing kills you (kept until you turn it off).

A new game always starts with an empty inventory (whatever is put in the scene for tests is removed).

All 10 levels were played through by an automatic test (doing what a player does for each goal) on 2026-10-28.

How to add a new level

  1. In Assets/Scripts/Story/StoryManager.cs, in the LevelsEn list (and the same in LevelsRo, in Romanian), add a new Level { … } before the level "THE RAFT" (the raft stays last, now level 10). It has: the title, the story lines, the steps, a hint and the closing text.
  2. In the Status() function each level has a case with its number, which says which steps are done. Add a case for the new level and move the numbers of the levels after it up by 1 (the raft uses Levels.Length, so it moves by itself).
  3. If the level needs to remember something (like "read the journal"), add a field to StoryData.cs; it is saved automatically.
  4. The Help screen reads the levels straight from StoryManager, it doesn't need changing.

Good to know

  • The .meta files are ignored by git: if you move the project to another computer, the scene's references are lost. Copy the whole folder, not only what is in git.
  • The scene is about 46 MB (many nature objects).
  • On Android the terrain textures must be ETC2 RGB (no alpha), otherwise the ground under the forests looks shiny / greenish.
  • The Enviroment object is not at the world's origin: new objects put under it need positions in world coordinates.
  • The game's icon: Assets/ART/Icons/AppIcon.png (and the Android version, with a separate background).
  • In the editor (not playing) the terrain uses Unity's own shader: the rock of the mountains shows only when the game runs.
